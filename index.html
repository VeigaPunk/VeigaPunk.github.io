Surface life

Domes, hyperloops & landing fields

Metro civilization thrives inside bio-domes; the wild green belt beyond is linked by rail and pad.

Bio-domes

The population lives mainly in bio-domes — climate-tamed urban and metropolitan habitats of chrome, glass, and soft tropical light. Civic life, leisure, and ceremony unfold under the curve of the canopy.

Hyperloop network

Outside the domes, a hyperloop system stitches landing pads and surface sites together — efficient transit across grasslands and river country without sprawling the cities into the wilderness.

Landing field three

Among known points of interest: Landing field three for planetary approach, and the Royal Hall sub-dome — ceremonial heart of the co-rulers’ court within the larger dome complex.

Species on-world (seen): Human, Ugnaught, Bith, Ishi Tib, Ithorian, Mon Calamari, Quarren, Querm Rybet, Rodian, Snivvian, Sullustan, and others — a pluralistic citizenry matching the charter’s ideals.

Polity

Elective monarchy & direct democracy

In the New Republic Era: co-rule by ballot and crown, pacifist charter, and a droid labor foundation.

How power works

  • Elective monarchy paired with direct democracy — citizens vote through automated systems rather than distant legislatures alone.
  • Co-rulers: the Duchess and Captain Bombardier (a former Imperial) share leadership after the planet’s recovery from harsh Galactic Empire rule.
  • Charter: pacifistic and pluralistic. Plazir-15 stands independent of the New Republic (off the NR registry) while maintaining its own restricted defense.
  • Labor & leisure: a large droid labor pool, maintained by Ugnaughts, underwrites lives of leisure for the citizenry.

Charter pillars

  1. Pacifism with restricted self-defense
  2. Pluralism across species & culture
  3. Direct citizen ballot via automation
  4. Independence from New Republic registry
  5. Droid labor as civic infrastructure

Ugnaught maintainers keep the droid pool operational — the quiet machinery of a leisure utopia.

Timeline

From Imperial yoke to recovery

  1. Galactic Empire

    Plazir-15 endured harsh Imperial rule — a chapter the modern charter and co-rule deliberately answer with pacifism, pluralism, and local sovereignty.

  2. New Republic Era recovery

    After the Empire’s fall, the world rebuilds as an elective monarchy with direct democracy. Citizens reclaim leisure and civic voice under automated voting systems.

  3. The Duchess & Captain Bombardier

    Co-rulers embody the dual inheritance of the age: noble continuity and a former Imperial who turned toward the planet’s charter future. Together they host guests in the Royal Hall sub-dome and welcome travelers into the “domed paradise.”
